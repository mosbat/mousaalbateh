I'm happy to announce launching my new personal blog.

I was inspired to create my own personal website when LinkedIn asked me to send them my passport to verify my account. This raised real concerns regarding our privacy and data.

Fast forward, I created finally my website where you can find information about me as well as professional background instead of LinkedIn.

I can argue that there is not much of a difference between having your profile on a personal website and LinkedIn with the latter being slightly worse in terms of privacy and lack of transparency.

My website will be the main source of truth in regards to content I post about tech, advocacy and other topics; and I hope that in future everyone will be able to create their own website and have sovereignty over their digital representation on the web.

Please note that I do not represent any organization.
